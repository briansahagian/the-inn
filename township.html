The township has much to offer.

Folks come through everyday from each corner of this country, and of course they're of great interest. However the real stars of the show are those who stay. Offering up invaluable goods and services, unmatched in expertise and skill, for prices even a bard could afford!

These are the folk that make up this town.

Ardavius The Archer, who supplies the town with ample and hearty provisions. Rabbit, turkey, venison - you name it, he'll get it.

Marvelus The Mage, who keeps the townsfolk free of illness and misfortune. Need a specialty antidote brewed in a blink? He's got you covered.

Saloman The Swordhand, who has defended the borders of this municipality for many moons. He is a swift solution for your most precarious for. Be it haughty hobgoblins or snappy sneeds, he does not falter.
